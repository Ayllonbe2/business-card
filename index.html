Leadership Approach

Three principles tested against a real portfolio, a real team and real investment decisions.

Build the bench, not just the backlog

Doubled the team through deliberate hiring, individual career paths and coaching of the managers who report into the role — including the hard conversations around underperformance rather than routing around them, because a team's ceiling is set by its weakest link.

Decide with evidence, and be willing to kill your own product

Recommended closing a platform I owned once the ROI and adoption data no longer supported it, and transferred another to a better-fit owner — both decisions defended with numbers in front of senior leadership.

Translate between worlds

Comfortable challenging model choices with ML engineers in the morning and framing the same problem as a business case for executives in the afternoon; this is where most analytics value is won or lost.
