Data scientist turned AI/ML leader. Over the past five years at BASF Digital Solutions I scaled a team from 8 to 16 data scientists, ML and software engineers and solution designers, and owned two production AI/ML platforms covering the full analytics journey from descriptive to predictive — delivering recurring savings above €3M. The technical depth is not decorative. Leadership Approach

Three principles that have survived contact with a real portfolio, a real team and a real restructuring.

Build the bench, not just the backlog

Doubled the team through deliberate hiring, individual career paths and coaching of the managers who report into the role — including the hard conversations around underperformance rather than routing around them.

Decide with evidence, and be willing to kill your own product

Recommended closing a platform I owned once the ROI and adoption data no longer supported it, and transferred another to a better-fit owner — both decisions defended with numbers in front of senior leadership.

Translate between worlds

Comfortable challenging model choices with ML engineers in the morning and framing the same problem as a business case for executives in the afternoon; this is where most analytics value is won or lost.
